Highlighted Projects

John B. DeValles Elementary School

Work

We helped the City explore how best to expand and modernize the school—studying different sites and building options to support growing enrollment while still keeping the school rooted in the neighborhood community.

Impact

This project will bring a modern, healthy, and sustainable school to local families—cutting energy use dramatically, aiming for Net Zero, and creating a long-term benefit for students, taxpayers, and the environment.

Leahy Elementary School

Work

We supported the creation of a new, modern K–8 school by managing construction on a tight urban site, coordinating phased demolition, and working closely with the neighborhood to keep the project moving smoothly and on schedule.

Impact

This new school will bring a vibrant, inclusive learning environment to over 1,000 students—complete with innovative classroom spaces, arts and STEM programs, accessible rooftop play areas, and community amenities—enhancing educational opportunities for families for years to come.

Shaker Lane Elementary School

Work

We are partnering with the Town to guide the planning and construction of a new state-of-the-art elementary school—overseeing design development, coordinating logistics for replacing the existing building, and ensuring the project meets educational goals and MSBA standards.

Impact

This new school will create a welcoming, future-ready learning environment for Littleton’s youngest students, with dedicated spaces for arts, play, and early childhood development—while improving safety, traffic flow, and community access for generations to come.
