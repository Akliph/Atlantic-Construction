About Atlantic

Founded in 2010 by Suresh Bhatia, Atlantic is a certified Minority Business Enterprise (MBE) specializing in Owner’s Project Management (OPM) for public sector construction projects across Massachusetts. Our mission is to deliver exceptional OPM services with integrity — ensuring that every publicly funded project is completed on time, on budget, and to the highest standards of quality and transparency. We have extensive experience with Massachusetts public procurement requirements, having managed taxpayer-funded projects since our inception. Even before founding Atlantic, Mr. Bhatia delivered MSBA/SBA school projects as a General Contractor, providing our firm with a well-rounded understanding of both the owner’s and builder’s perspectives.


Selecting an OPM is more than hiring a consultant — it’s choosing a strategic partner who can integrate community engagement, financial planning, stakeholder coordination, procurement, and long-term project oversight into one cohesive process. Our leadership team — Project Director Suresh Bhatia and Senior Project Manager Jennifer dos Santos — are career project management professionals with decades of experience guiding complex public projects. Jennifer brings valuable insight from her work in a design, commissioning and project management firm and is especially skilled at building trust and consensus among diverse stakeholder groups, from municipal leaders to community members.
